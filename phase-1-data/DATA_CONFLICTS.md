Conflict: 2022-23 source records nominal maturity as 155 days under standard sowing; 2024-25 emphasizes shifting the sowing window earlier by 15-20 days to mitigate terminal heat.
Possible explanation: Early sowing extends the vegetative phase and total calendar days to maturity depending on winter temperature regimes.

Source: ICAR Annual Report 2023-24, Page 33, Section: Crop Improvement - Oilseeds

Value: Mentioned under Frontline Demonstrations with average pod yield increase of 26.67% over local checks without seasonal maturity breakdown.
Source: ICAR Annual Report 2024-25, Page 185, Section: Technology Assessment and Demonstration

Conflict: Multi-season differential maturity (kharif 100-105 days vs rabi 105-110 days) exists within the same cultivar.
